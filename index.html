Who I Am

I'm Shubaib, an aspiring AI Engineer from Pakistan focused on building intelligent systems that solve real-world problems through Generative and Agentic AI.

I work with technologies like RAG, Corrective RAG, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) to create AI systems that retrieve, reason, and respond with accuracy and context awareness.

My journey is completely self-taught, driven by curiosity, consistency, and building real-world projects from scratch. I enjoy transforming complex AI concepts into practical, scalable applications.

Currently, I'm focused on becoming a full-stack AI Engineer in the new era of Generative & Agentic AI — combining intelligent backends, automation, and modern user experiences into production-ready systems.
